CORPORATE AI INVESTMENT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Classification: Training Example

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE

This policy establishes the approval requirements
for proposed AI investments.

SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THRESHOLDS

AI investments below $25,000 require approval
from the department manager.

AI investments from $25,000 through $100,000
require approval from the business unit director.

AI investments exceeding $100,000 require
executive investment committee approval.

SECTION 3 — SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

All AI projects must complete an information
security review before production deployment.

Projects involving sensitive customer information
require additional data-handling approval.

SECTION 4 — BUSINESS CASE REQUIREMENTS

All proposed AI investments must document
implementation costs, recurring operating costs,
expected benefits, and principal risks.

Projects exceeding $50,000 must include
a documented return-on-investment analysis.
